## About 

I'm a third-year honors student in computer science and math at Clemson University, currently affiliated with [DevLab@Clemson](https://clemsondevlab.com/) under [Dr. Paige Rodeghero](https://paigerodeghero.com/) and [Dr. Jonathan Aldrich](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~aldrich/)'s research group at Carnegie Mellon University.

I also co-direct [CUhackit](https://cuhack.it/#/), the student organization hosting Clemson's annual hackathons.

## Research

I'm interested in making it easier for developers to build safe software using techniques from programming languages, software engineering, and human-computer interaction.

During the summer of 2025, I participated in the [REUSE](https://www.cmu.edu/scs/s3d/reuse/) program at Carnegie Mellon University where I worked with Long Nguyen and [Dr. Jonathan Aldrich](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~aldrich/) to extend gradual verification.
